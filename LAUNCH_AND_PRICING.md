# Veyra commercial concept

These prices are launch hypotheses for customer interviews and a paid pilot, not validated market rates or earnings promises. The app does not sell subscriptions yet.

| Proposed plan | Price | Intended buyer | Future paid benefit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | Free | Guest or organizer trying the experience | Small trial gallery and essential sharing |
| Signature | ₹1,499 per event | Independent wedding photographer | 5,000 photos, 30-day hosting, two team members, consented AI discovery and branded delivery |
| Studio | ₹4,999 per month | Repeat-event photography studio | Five active events, 25,000 new photos/month, 90-day hosting, five team members, studio branding and analytics |

Define active-event limits and retention precisely before sale. Avoid unlimited storage or messaging promises. Guests should generally browse/download free; sell organizer convenience, team workflow, presentation, predictable hosting, and faster delivery. Later test additional storage, longer retention, print fulfillment and agency branding separately.

At 20 Signature sales/month, illustrative gross sales are **20 × ₹1,499 = ₹29,980/month** before refunds, taxes, platform fees, storage, bandwidth, recognition, messaging, support, and acquisition costs. This is not profit or a sales forecast.

Track contribution per event as: net collected revenue minus storage, egress, processing, delivery, and direct support costs. Divide fixed monthly costs by positive contribution per event to estimate break-even event count. Measure real upload sizes, downloads per guest, match-processing costs, and retention before finalizing quotas.

Run a pilot with 5–10 photographers across smaller events and multi-day weddings. Record time-to-first-gallery, upload failure rate, guest completion rate, uncertain/incorrect match reports, cost per event, support minutes, and willingness to pay. Require a clear match-quality and deletion standard before enabling face-based delivery to real guests.

For digital features sold in the iOS app, plan around Apple's current in-app-purchase requirements and applicable exceptions. Implement StoreKit purchase restoration and server-verified entitlements; do not treat a selected plan or client-side preference as payment proof. Review the [official App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) before release. Recheck pricing, regional terms, applicable taxes and legal obligations at launch.
